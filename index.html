How to play

Eggs roll down four ramps. Move the basket to the end of a ramp before its egg gets there.

QUpper left
EUpper right
ALower left
DLower right

Arrow keys also work: left and right pick a side, up and down pick a ramp. On a phone, tap the four arrows.

White eggs score 1. Golden eggs score 5, appear 5% of the time, and fall twice as fast. Three misses end the round. Eggs speed up every ten points.
